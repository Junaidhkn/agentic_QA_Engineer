# Terms of Service

> **Draft for legal review — not yet in force.** Version 2026-10-25.

## 1. The service
Reliability Engineer ("we") tests web applications you are authorized to test: it discovers
workflows, runs functional and load tests, detects performance and correctness problems,
proposes root-cause hypotheses with evidence, and can propose code changes as pull requests.
Results are produced partly by automated analysis and AI models.

## 2. Your account
You are responsible for activity under your organization, its members and API keys. Keep
credentials secret and enable multi-factor authentication for owners and admins. You must be
able to bind your organization to these terms.

## 3. Acceptable use
Your use is subject to the [Acceptable Use Policy](aup.md), which is part of these terms. In
particular you may only point tests at systems you own or are authorized to test.

## 4. Your data
You keep all rights to your code, test definitions, results and other content ("Customer
Data"). You grant us the rights needed to operate the service for you: storing it, running
tests, sending relevant excerpts to the AI model provider listed as a sub-processor, and
creating pull requests you approve. We do not use Customer Data to train models. Retention and
deletion follow the [Data Processing Addendum](dpa.md); you can delete your organization at any
time.

## 5. AI-generated output
Hypotheses, generated tests and proposed patches can be wrong. They are labelled as such,
backed by the evidence shown, and code changes are only proposed after the verification gates
listed with them pass and a person in your organization approves. You remain responsible for
reviewing and merging changes. We never merge or deploy to your systems.

## 6. Plans, limits and fees
Plans set limits on virtual users, durations, concurrency, sandbox minutes and AI usage. Paid
plans are billed monthly in advance; usage above plan limits is refused, not billed. We may
change prices with 30 days' notice.

## 7. Suspension and termination
We may suspend an organization immediately to stop harm (for example traffic against systems
you are not authorized to test) and will tell you why. You may stop using the service at any
time. On termination we delete Customer Data as described in the DPA.

## 8. Availability and support
We aim for the availability objectives published on our status page; they are objectives,
not guarantees, unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

## 9. Warranties and liability
The service is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, our total liability is
limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim, and neither party is liable
for indirect or consequential damages. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Changes
We will notify owners of material changes at least 30 days before they take effect; you will
be asked to accept the new version before running further tests.
